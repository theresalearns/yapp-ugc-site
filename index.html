FAQ

Fair questions.

How is this different from HeyGen or Argil?

Those are tools. You still write the scripts, pick the avatar and render it yourself — and the likeness is either yours or stock. We run the production: brief in, cast assigned, variations written and rendered, files back. Every face in our pool has a signed, revocable license behind it.

How is this different from a UGC marketplace?

A marketplace sells you access to one creator for one gig, then the relationship leaves the platform. We sell finished volume — 60 videos from one brief — and the economics work because the avatars render rather than film.

Who owns the creator's likeness?

The creator. Always. Brands license usage per campaign, platform and period. Creators see every use in the ledger and can pause or revoke, which stops new renders platform-wide.

Will this pass ad review?

Files ship with AI-content disclosure metadata and labels, which is what TikTok and Meta ask for on synthetic media. For regulated categories we hold claim rules per brand so scripts stay compliant.

What do I get on day one?

A production plan: the cast, the hook angles, the script directions and a delivery date. Free. You decide from there.
